Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

PRAVDA ("we", "us", "our") is a creative and marketing services agency based in Amman, Jordan. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to the enquiries and services we handle through it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, company name, and anything else you include when you contact us by WhatsApp, email, or social media.
  • Client project material. Content, brand assets, and account access you provide so that we can deliver agreed services.
  • Technical information. Basic data your browser sends when visiting this site, such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you ask for.
  • To manage and improve our client relationships and internal operations.
  • To operate advertising and social media accounts on behalf of clients who have authorised us to do so.
  • To meet legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

3. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

4. Third-party platforms

We use third-party platforms to deliver our services, including Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google, TikTok, and WhatsApp. When you interact with us through those platforms, their own privacy policies also apply to that interaction. We do not control how those companies process your data.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (such as hosting, communications, and advertising platforms), with a client where the information relates directly to work we perform for them, or where we are required to do so by law.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, and for any period required by Jordanian law. Project material is retained for the duration of the engagement and a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted or archived.

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period.

9. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
